## Summary

Adopts six invariants as the hard floor of the EcoHubs governance system. While in force, no decision, role, process, or emergency measure may override them; where an invariant conflicts with any other rule, the invariant prevails.

## Adopted Content

### Active Invariants

| ID | Invariant |
|---|---|
| INV-001 | The primary purpose may never be overridden by any operational or strategic decision, role, or emergency measure. |
| INV-002 | Authority must always be explicit and traceable to the governance system — no person or role may hold or exercise undeclared authority. |
| INV-003 | Exit from the community must always be possible and must never be blocked, penalised, or made conditional beyond what is defined in Layer 1. |
| INV-004 | The structural layer must remain non-ideological — no belief system, culture, or ideology may be embedded as a requirement in RCOS Core or EcoHubs governance. |
| INV-005 | EcoHubs may never be used as a vehicle for speculative financial gain or extraction from its members. |
| INV-006 | Physical, psychological, and child safety always override participation rights, role continuity, and reputational concerns. |

## Rationale

RCOS-Core §2.3 requires invariants that cannot be overridden while in force. Rules that can be suspended under pressure are preferences, not constraints — and a governance system is only trustworthy if some things hold on its worst day, not just its calm ones. These six bind the purpose (INV-001), authority (INV-002), exit (INV-003), non-ideology (INV-004), non-extraction (INV-005), and safety (INV-006) across every layer.

## Risks and Mitigations

- **Risk:** An invariant proves unworkable and cannot be quickly relaxed. **Mitigation:** Invariants can be changed by Constitutional decision; the difficulty is intentional and bounded, not absolute.
- **Risk:** INV-006 (safety overrides) could be invoked to bypass due process. **Mitigation:** The Accountability Protocol and Conflict Resolution Ladder (Layer 4) define the process by which safety concerns are raised and handled, including anti-retaliation provisions.

## Transition and Migration Plan

No migration required. Status changes from *Stub — not yet adopted* to *Active — adopted 2026-07-03*. All six invariants take effect on the effective date and are recorded in the register with that date.

## Rollback Plan

Rollback requires a further Constitutional decision removing or amending specific invariants. Rollback trigger: a ratified finding that an invariant is unenforceable or in irreconcilable conflict with the primary purpose.
