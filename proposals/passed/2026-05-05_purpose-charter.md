## Summary

Adopts the Purpose Charter as the first ratified Layer 0 artifact. It fixes the primary purpose of EcoHubs, declares three subordinate secondary purposes, names four things EcoHubs explicitly is not, and locks the primary purpose behind a Constitutional decision with a 30-day ratification period.

## Adopted Content

### Primary Purpose

To make regenerative community living — rooted in care for people, land, and life — structurally accessible, resilient, and replicable, reducing humanity's dependency on extractive systems one community at a time.

### Secondary Purposes

1. To develop and maintain reusable foundations — tools, standards, and resources — that lower the barriers to starting, sustaining, and replicating regenerative communities.
2. To demonstrate through lived practice that regenerative community living is viable, replicable, and worth choosing.
3. To grow a distributed network of communities that support, learn from, and strengthen each other.

### Non-Goals and Exclusions

1. EcoHubs is not a political party or ideological movement.
2. EcoHubs is not a for-profit real estate or investment vehicle.
3. EcoHubs is not a single prescribed way of living — it provides structure, not answers.
4. EcoHubs is not a short-term project collective or event-based community.

### Conditions for Purpose Change

The primary purpose may only be changed through a Constitutional decision as defined in the Decision Matrix (Layer 2), requiring a supermajority of Full Members (≥⅔ of votes cast), followed by a ratification period of no less than 30 days. Secondary purposes may be changed through a Strategic decision. Any change must be recorded in the Version History (Layer 6).

## Rationale

RCOS-Core §2.1 requires a community to define exactly one primary purpose that describes the enduring reason for its existence, is stable across time, and can only be changed constitutionally. Without a ratified purpose, no downstream artifact — scope, invariants, governance, or economy — has an anchor to be consistent with. Adopting the Purpose Charter is the precondition for every other Layer 0 artifact.

## Risks and Mitigations

- **Risk:** A purpose fixed this early may prove too narrow as the network grows. **Mitigation:** Secondary purposes carry the evolving work and can be changed by Strategic decision; the primary purpose remains deliberately broad.
- **Risk:** A constitutionally locked purpose slows legitimate adaptation. **Mitigation:** This is the intended trade-off — §2.1.3 requires it. The re-vote route in the Governance Protocol remains open to any Full Member.

## Transition and Migration Plan

No migration required. The Purpose Charter status changes from *Stub — not yet adopted* to *Active — adopted 2026-07-03*; the artifact content is unchanged from the version deliberated.

## Rollback Plan

Rollback requires a further Constitutional decision reverting the artifact to its pre-adoption state. Rollback trigger: a ratified finding that the stated purpose is unachievable or has been superseded by a subsequent Constitutional decision.
